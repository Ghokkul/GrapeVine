Questions

Before you start.

What is actually in the directory?

Seven working apps: Brandofme.ai, Soul Weather Station, Axiom, Brain Dump, tracelight.ai, Immune and Cipherscope. Each one is hosted and open right now — the links in the list go straight to the live product.

Does it cost anything to try them?

Browsing Grapevine is free and nothing here asks for a card before you've used the product. Pricing, where it exists, lives on each app's own site.

Who made these?

All seven are built by Ghokkul M. That's why they share a look and why feedback goes straight to the person who can act on it.

What does "looking for customers" mean here?

These are new products past the build stage and short on users. Signing up early means your feedback shapes what ships next, which is the trade — you get in first, the app gets better.

Can I list my own app?

The directory is small on purpose, but it's growing. Get in touch and tell us what your app does in one sentence — that sentence is what the search runs on.

One of the seven is for you.

Pick the problem you had this week and open the app that handles it. Takes about a minute to find out.
