Questions

Before you start.

Do I need an audience to get listed?

No. There's no follower count, no user minimum, no traffic requirement. If your app is built and the link works, it can go in the directory. Grapevine is specifically for products that exist but haven't been found yet.

How do I get my app listed?

Fill in the form in the Get listed section. It sends your details to helloghokkul@gmail.com, and we reply within a few days. You can also email that address directly if you'd rather write it yourself.

What's actually in the directory?

Eight working apps: Fumble, Brandofme.ai, Soul Weather Station, Axiom, Brain Dump, tracelight.ai, Immune and Cipherscope. Each one is hosted and open right now — the links go straight to the live product.

What does “uncorked” mean?

Uncorked is our word for a project that decided to go public — built, hosted, and open to anyone, with nothing sitting behind a waitlist. Every app in the directory is uncorked. The first pour is Fumble, the first outside business to collaborate with Grapevine.

Does it cost anything?

Listing is free and browsing is free. Nothing here asks for a card before you've used a product. Pricing, where it exists, lives on each app's own site.

What does “looking for customers” mean here?

Every listed app is past the build stage and short on users. Signing up early means your feedback shapes what ships next — you get in first, the app gets better.

Who runs Grapevine?

Grapevine is run by Ghokkul M. The seven starter apps were built by Ninth Sun Studios in partnership with Grapevine. Fumble, built in New Zealand, is the first outside business to join the vine — the first pour. For anything not answered here, write to helloghokkul@gmail.com.
